# City View Park & Resort: public information for the demo

Source: public listings on Nigeria Property Centre and PrivateProperty.ng (as of Nov 2026).
These are agents' marketing descriptions, NOT confirmed by the developer.
The chatbot must present them as "listed" information and let the agent confirm.

## What it is
- A residential and mixed-use LAND estate (plots, not built houses). Buy-and-build.
- Listings describe it as a gated estate with a hilltop, city-view setting.

## Location (listings disagree, so the agent must confirm exact location)
- Most listings: Gousa District, Abuja (near Idu, Lugbe, Central Business District axis).
- At least one listing: Gwagwalada, Abuja.
- Landmarks named in listings: University of Abuja, Immigration Office, Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport, Idu Train Station, WAEC Office.

## Plot sizes mentioned in listings
- 200 sqm, 300 sqm, 500 sqm, 1,000 sqm (including a commercial plot option), 1 acre.
- Both residential and commercial plots are mentioned.

## Title (as stated in listings, not verified)
- Listings say FCDA approval and a Certificate of Occupancy (C of O).
- The chatbot must NOT confirm title as fact. Buyers should have the agent provide documents for independent verification.

## Features and amenities (as stated in listings)
- Gate house, perimeter fencing, CCTV, security.
- Road and street lighting, landscaped garden, car park.
- Recreational centre, gym, mini golf, mini polo, helipad, electric car charging port, "AI hub".

## Prices
- Listings show very different prices for similar plots (from under 1 million up to about 30 million naira, depending on the listing, plot size and seller).
- The chatbot must NOT quote any price. It should say the agent will confirm current pricing and payment plans.

## Not known (agent must answer)
- Current availability, exact prices, payment plans, inspection schedule, survey and title documents, developer details.
